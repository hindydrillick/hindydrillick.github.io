I am currently a Courant Instructor at the [Courant Institute School of Mathematics, Computing, and Data Science(https://cims.nyu.edu). I recently finished up my PhD at [Columbia University](https://www.math.columbia.edu) under the supervision of [Ivan Corwin](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~corwin). My research mainly focuses on interacting particle systems and their scaling limits to solutions of stochastic partial differential equations.
